How does it work?

Roman numerals are written with I (1), V (5), X (10), L (50), C (100), D (500) and M (1000). Symbols are added from left to right, except when a smaller symbol precedes a larger one: it is then subtracted (IV = 4, IX = 9, XL = 40, XC = 90, CD = 400, CM = 900). Example: 2026 is written MMXXVI.

Limitations to know

  • The range 1 to 3999 matches the usual notation; larger numbers used special conventions (a bar over the letters).
  • Non-standard forms (such as IIII or VX) are rejected.
