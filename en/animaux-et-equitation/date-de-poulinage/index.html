Foaling date

Enter the covering date to get an estimated foaling date and the usual range.

Choose a date.

How does it work?

A mare’s pregnancy lasts about 340 days on average (a little under 11 months). The tool adds 340 days to the covering date and shows a range of 320 to 360 days, in which most normal pregnancies fall.

Advertisement

Limitations to know

  • The length varies with the mare, the breed, the covering season and the foal’s sex: a birth outside the range is not necessarily abnormal, but should be reported to the vet.
  • The date is an estimate: only your vet’s ultrasound follow-up can pinpoint the stage of pregnancy.
  • The tool does not replace a vet’s advice; it does not detect pregnancy problems or the best covering date.

The calculation is done in your browser: nothing is sent to a server. Content written without any particular external source; for an important decision, check with an official source.
