Compound interest calculator

Enter a starting balance, a monthly deposit, an annual return and a duration to simulate how your savings will grow.

—

How does it work?

The annual rate is converted into an equivalent monthly rate: i = (1 + rate)1/12 − 1. The final balance is C × (1 + i)n + V × ((1 + i)n − 1) ÷ i, where n is the number of months and V the monthly deposit (paid at the end of the month). Interest is the final balance minus the total paid in.

Limitations to know

  • A constant return is an assumption: real investments fluctuate and the capital can fall.
  • Taxes, social contributions, fees and inflation are not taken into account.
  • Indicative simulation, not investment advice.

The calculation is done in your browser: nothing is sent to a server. Content written without any particular external source; for an important decision, check with an official source.
