Color converter

Pick a color or enter its values: the HEX, RGB and HSL formats update together.

—

How does it work?

HEX writes the red, green and blue components in hexadecimal (#RRGGBB). RGB gives them in decimal from 0 to 255. HSL describes the color by its hue (an angle on the color wheel), its saturation and its lightness. Contrast is the luminance ratio defined by the WCAG accessibility rules: 4.5:1 is the recommended minimum for body text.

Limitations to know

  • Conversions are rounded to whole numbers; converting from HSL to RGB several times can shift a value by one.
  • The display depends on the screen: the same color does not look identical everywhere, and printing uses a different system (CMYK) that is not covered here.

The calculation is done in your browser: nothing is sent to a server. Content written without any particular external source; for an important decision, check with an official source.
