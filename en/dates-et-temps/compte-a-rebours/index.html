Countdown

Choose a date and a time: the countdown updates every second.

Choose a date and a time.

How does it work?

The countdown compares the chosen date with your device’s time, in your time zone, and refreshes every second while the page is open. Once the date has passed, the tool shows the time elapsed since then.

Advertisement

Limitations to know

  • The result depends on your device’s clock: if it is off, the countdown is too.
  • Daylight-saving changes are handled by the browser.
  • The countdown stops if you close the page; it does not send notifications.

The calculation is done in your browser: nothing is sent to a server. Content written without any particular external source; for an important decision, check with an official source.
